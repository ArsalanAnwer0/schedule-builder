# Getting Started with Schedule Builder

Welcome to Schedule Builder! This guide will help you understand the platform and get started quickly.

## What is Schedule Builder?

Schedule Builder is a modern team scheduling platform designed to simplify workforce management. Whether you're managing shifts for a small team or coordinating schedules across multiple departments, Schedule Builder provides the tools you need.

## Key Features

- **Smart Scheduling** - Create optimized schedules based on availability and preferences
- **Team Collaboration** - Keep your team in sync with real-time updates
- **Notifications** - Stay informed with email and in-app notifications
- **Reporting** - Track scheduling patterns and team availability
- **Security** - Your data is encrypted and GDPR/CCPA compliant

## Who Should Use Schedule Builder?

Schedule Builder is ideal for:
- Retail teams managing store shifts
- Healthcare facilities coordinating nursing schedules
- Restaurants and hospitality businesses
- Corporate teams organizing desk coverage
- Educational institutions scheduling faculty
- Non-profit organizations coordinating volunteers

## How It Works

1. **Create Your Organization** - Sign up and set up your team
2. **Add Team Members** - Invite students or secondary admins
3. **Build Schedules** - Create and optimize schedules
4. **Communicate** - Keep everyone informed with notifications
5. **Analyze** - Review reports and improve scheduling
