# Quick Start Guide

Get started with Schedule Builder in 5 minutes. This guide covers the essential steps to create your first schedule.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [schedule-builder.xyz/register](https://schedule-builder.xyz/register)
2. Enter your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Organization name
   - Password (must meet security requirements)
3. Click "Register" to create your account

::: tip Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. See [Password Requirements](/guide/password-requirements) for details.
:::

## Step 2: Access the Dashboard

After registration, you'll be redirected to your dashboard where you can:
- View your organization overview
- Manage team members
- Create schedules
- View notifications

## Step 3: Add Team Members

### Adding Students
1. Navigate to the Students section
2. Click "Invite Student"
3. Enter the student's email and name
4. Send the invitation

### Adding Secondary Admins
1. Navigate to the Admin Team section (Primary Admin only)
2. Click "Invite Admin"
3. Enter the admin's email
4. Send the invitation

Team members will receive an email with instructions to set their password and access the system.

## Step 4: Request Availability

1. Click "Request Availability" from the dashboard
2. Select the date range for scheduling
3. The system will notify all students to submit their availability

## Step 5: Create a Schedule

1. Wait for students to submit their availability
2. Navigate to the Schedule section
3. Click "Generate Schedule"
4. The system will create an optimized schedule based on:
   - Student availability
   - Scheduling preferences
   - Business rules

## Step 6: Publish and Notify

Once you're satisfied with the schedule:
1. Review the generated schedule
2. Make any manual adjustments if needed
3. Click "Publish Schedule"
4. All team members will receive notifications

## Common First-Time Questions

**Q: Can I edit a schedule after publishing?**
A: Yes, you can make changes to published schedules. Team members will be notified of updates.

**Q: How do students submit availability?**
A: Students receive a notification and can log in to submit their available time slots.

**Q: What if a student forgets their password?**
A: They can use the "Forgot Password" feature. Primary admins can approve password reset requests.

**Q: Can I export schedules?**
A: Yes, schedules can be exported in various formats from the Schedule section.
