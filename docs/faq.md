# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Schedule Builder. Can't find what you're looking for? Visit our [Contact page](/legal/contact) for more support options.

## General Questions

### What is Schedule Builder?

Schedule Builder is a web-based team scheduling platform that helps managers create, manage, and optimize work schedules for their teams. It automates the scheduling process, prevents conflicts, and keeps everyone informed with smart notifications.

### Is Schedule Builder free?

Schedule Builder offers both free and paid plans:
- **Free Plan**: Perfect for small teams, includes core scheduling features
- **Pro Plan**: Advanced features, more users, priority support
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom solutions for large organizations

Visit our [pricing page](https://schedule-builder.xyz) for current pricing details.

### Do I need to install any software?

No! Schedule Builder is a web-based application that works in your browser. Simply create an account and start scheduling - no downloads or installations required.

### What browsers are supported?

Schedule Builder works best on modern browsers including:
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

We recommend using the latest version of your browser for the best experience.

## Account & Security

### How do I create an account?

Visit [schedule-builder.xyz/register](https://schedule-builder.xyz/register) and follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address and create a password
2. Verify your email address
3. Complete your profile
4. Start creating schedules!

### Is my data secure?

Yes! We take security seriously:
- All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
- Regular security audits and updates
- GDPR and CCPA compliant

Learn more in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

### Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can delete your account at any time:
1. Go to Settings > Account
2. Click "Delete Account"
3. Confirm your decision
4. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

## Using Schedule Builder

### How do I create a schedule?

Creating a schedule is easy:
1. Click "New Schedule" from your dashboard
2. Add team members
3. Set shift times and requirements
4. Use auto-generate or manually assign shifts
5. Publish to notify your team

Check our [User Guide](/guide/creating-schedules) for detailed instructions.

### Can team members request time off?

Yes! Team members can:
- Submit time-off requests through their account
- Managers receive notifications and can approve/deny requests
- Approved time-off is automatically considered in schedule generation

### How do notifications work?

Schedule Builder sends notifications for:
- Schedule publications and changes
- Shift reminders
- Time-off request updates
- Important announcements

You can customize notification preferences in your account settings.

## Troubleshooting

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

1. Go to the [login page](https://schedule-builder.xyz/login)
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for reset instructions
5. Create a new password

### Why can't I see my schedule?

If you can't see your schedule, check:
- You're logged into the correct account
- Your manager has published the schedule
- You have the correct permissions
- Your internet connection is working

Still having issues? Visit our [Troubleshooting page](/troubleshooting/).

### The site isn't loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Refresh the page (Cmd/Ctrl + R)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check [status page] for known issues
5. Contact [support](/legal/contact) if the problem persists

## Billing & Subscriptions

### How does billing work?

- **Monthly plans** are billed on the same day each month
- **Annual plans** are billed once per year with a discount
- You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime
- Refunds available within 30 days (see [Terms](/legal/terms))

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- Enterprise customers: Invoice and wire transfer options available

### Can I change my plan?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time:
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Change Plan"
3. Select your new plan
4. Confirm the change

**Upgrades** take effect immediately. **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing period.
